# Website photography

The current hero is one finished outdoor portrait edited from the user's photographs with the built-in imagegen tool. The subject and setting share a single image; the website no longer overlays a person cutout onto a separate background. The outdoor setting and supporting photographs are illustrative, not claims of completed client work. Final assets were optimized to WebP with Sharp.

## Current integrated outdoor portrait

File: `public/assets/images/handyman-outdoor.webp` (opaque WebP, 1200 x 1200).

The wider original photograph is the edit target, the close-up is an identity reference, and the house image is a materials/color reference. The edit integrates outdoor lighting, white balance, shadows, perspective, edge detail and depth of field into one photograph. The existing small hover lift/zoom remains on this image and the two supporting photographs.

Built-in imagegen prompt:

> Use case: identity-preserve / photorealistic environmental portrait edit. Image 1 is the edit target and the person's full pose, clothing, tattoos and tool belt reference. Image 2 is a close-up reference for the SAME man's exact face and identity. Image 3 is ONLY a visual reference for the house color and materials, not a backdrop to paste him over. Produce a SINGLE cohesive camera photograph of THIS SAME handyman actually standing on the walkway immediately in front of a modest sage-green house. This must look like one real outdoor portrait captured by a photographer, not a cutout collage. Preserve the man's recognizable face, facial geometry, hair, age, natural expression, body build, crossed-arm pose, black work T-shirt, blue jeans, existing tattoo patterns and work belt. Relight his face, exposed skin, shirt, tools and hair together for the outdoor setting: soft overcast daylight from camera-left, natural cooler outdoor white balance, subtle reflected green from the garden, believable gentle shadows under chin and arms. Remove the warm orange indoor lighting from the target photo. Integrate fine hair and skin edges naturally into the scene, no halo, crisp outline, sticker edges or separate portrait layer. Match subject and architecture camera perspective, light direction, optical depth, color response and fine photographic grain throughout. Camera at the man's eye level, 45mm full-frame lens at f/4, subject about 2.5 metres from camera and the porch about 1.5 metres behind him. Keep the house believable in scale and modestly soft but recognisable, with off-white trim, an oak door and charcoal-framed windows. Realistic documentary small-business portrait, restrained green and neutral palette, no heavy cinematic grade, HDR or glamour retouching. Square 1:1 output, show from above the top of his head to below the tool belt, subject centered, leave roughly 10 percent clear headroom and enough space beside both elbows for a slightly wide website crop. Keep the person's anatomy, identity and tattoos faithful. No additional people, no added tools, no text, no logo, no watermark. The result should be an opaque, finished outdoor photograph with its own fully integrated background.
